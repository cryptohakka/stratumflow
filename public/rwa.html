risk context
Bybit Hack (Feb 21, 2025) — A Real Stress Test: The Lazarus Group stole 15,000 cmETH and 8,000 mETH from Bybit's cold wallet. The 8,000 mETH were immediately swapped to ETH via DEX across 3 transactions — DEX liquidity was sufficient and the exit completed. The 15,000 cmETH conversion was left incomplete due to on-chain liquidity constraints. The 8-hour redemption delay built into mETH Protocol gave the team time to pause the contract and recover all 15,000 cmETH (~$43M).

Key insight: Direct redemption (cmETH → mETH → ETH) is not an emergency exit — it is a delayed mechanism. DEX exit depth is the only real-time liquidity indicator during stress events. A $500K cmETH position on Mantle currently incurs — price impact to exit via USDC. StratumFlow monitors this depth continuously and biases toward risk-off allocation when $100K-tier impact exceeds 3%.
